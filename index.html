How to Play

First Light is a growing-up adventure. You begin as a very young kit or pup, so the world opens gradually.

Moving around

Use the round joystick in the lower-left corner to move. When you get close enough to a character, den, or other interactive place, the paw button will glow.

Talking & playing

Walk near another character and tap the glowing paw. You can talk, learn who they are, and sometimes play, stalk, wrestle, eat, or do other activities. These actions improve skills and relationships.

Days & growing up

Your hunger and energy slowly fall during the day. Return to your sleeping place and choose Sleep to begin a new day. New events, areas, skills, and story moments unlock as days pass.

Finding characters

Pack and Colony members have routines. They may be in different places depending on the time of day. If someone is not where you last saw them, look around their usual areas.

Exploration & the map

The map starts covered in fog because you are still little. Exploring reveals important places. After your first night, listen for clues about something beyond the familiar paths...

The waterfall

Starting on Day 2, a new exploration objective appears. Follow the clue toward the northwest / upper-left edge of your home area. When you get close enough, the waterfall discovers itself and is added to your map.

What happens later?

First Light begins with family, play, and learning your home. As you grow, you will learn to hunt, track or stalk, explore farther, patrol borders, meet animals outside your own society, and make choices that affect relationships and the story.

Tip: There is no need to rush. Talking to characters, playing with siblings, exploring, eating, and sleeping are all part of progressing.
